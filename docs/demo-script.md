# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

**0:00 Hook.** "Ever promised yourself you'd hit the gym and didn't? What if breaking that promise cost you real money, and paid your friend?"

**0:15 Stake.** Connect Phantom. Goal: "drink a glass of water". Stake 0.05 SOL. Deadline: 3 minutes (demo). Friend: second wallet. Approve in Phantom. Click "view tx" and show the memo with the goal on Solana Explorer.

**0:45 Fake proof.** Upload an unrelated photo (a keyboard). Referee rejects it with a witty reason. Point out: rejection is not a forfeit, retry until the deadline.

**1:05 Real proof.** Upload a photo of you holding a glass of water. Referee passes it. Refund lands. Open the payout tx: memo holds the verdict and the reason.

**1:25 Broken promise.** Show a second goal (staked earlier) whose 3 minutes are up. Press "Pay the friend". Friend's wallet receives the SOL. Card turns red and gets struck through on the wall.

**1:45 Architecture.** "No database. Goals and verdicts are memos on Solana. The server reads stake facts from the chain, so the client cannot lie, and it only trusts settlements signed by escrow."

**1:55 Close.** "Skin in the Game. Put money on your word."

Prep: stake the "broken" goal about 4 minutes before recording. Have two Phantom accounts with devnet SOL.
